    How to play
    
    
    Three words were riffled together like a deck of cards. Each word kept its letters in order, but they got mixed in with each other.
    
    
    
      cdaotg
    
    
    
    CAT and DOG, riffled.
    
    
      
      
    • Tap letters left to right to spell a word. You can skip as many letters as you like.
      • 
      
    • Every word needs at least 3 letters. Any word counts, not just the hidden ones.
      • 
      
    • Each letter can be used once. Clear the whole strip to win.
      • 
      
    • Fewer words is better. Par is 3. If you find a long word that wasn't hidden, you can finish under par.
      • 
      
    • Short, easy words can leave you with letters you can't use. Tap × on a word to put its letters back.
      • 
    
    
    
    A new puzzle every day. Typing on a keyboard works too.
